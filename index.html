Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone walks in hot -- a frustrated customer, a kid mid-meltdown, whoever -- don't match their volume. Drop your voice half a register and slow your cadence. It signals control without a single word about who's in charge, and it pulls their energy down to meet yours instead of the other way around.

Exercise: Today, the next three times someone comes at you frustrated or short, pause two full seconds before you respond, then answer quieter and slower than they did. Notice whether their volume drops to match yours by the end of the exchange.
