Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's heated, resist the urge to correct them first. Mirror back what you heard in one plain sentence before you say anything else -- people de-escalate the moment they feel understood, not when they feel corrected.

Exercise: In your next three tense or pointed conversations today -- a customer, a staffer, one of the kids -- paraphrase what they said back to them before you respond with your own point. Let them confirm or fix it first, then watch how much the temperature drops before you even get to your answer.
