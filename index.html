Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes at you hot, drop your volume instead of matching theirs — a calm, lower voice forces them to quiet down just to hear you, and it keeps you looking like the adult in the room.

Exercise: Next time someone's frustrated with you today (a customer, a kid, an employee), consciously lower your voice half a notch before you respond and open with a question instead of a comeback. Notice what it does to their tone.
