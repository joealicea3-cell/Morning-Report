Verbal Judo

Tip: Swap 'why' questions for 'what' or 'how' when someone's heated - 'why' sounds like an accusation and makes people defensive, while 'what happened' or 'how can we fix this' invites them to explain without feeling attacked.

Exercise: In your next three tense conversations today (customer, student, or one of the kids), catch yourself before asking 'why' and reframe it as a 'what' or 'how' question instead - count how many times you catch it.
