Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes in hot, before you respond, silently count two full seconds. That pause kills the reflex to match their heat, and it reads to them as control, not weakness. It's the difference between reacting and choosing.

Exercise: Today, pick one tense or annoying exchange, a customer complaint, a kid pushing back, whatever shows up, and force the two-second pause before you answer. Tonight, jot one line on how the outcome changed versus your normal instant reply.
