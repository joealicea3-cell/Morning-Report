Verbal Judo

Tip: Use the core Verbal Judo move: paraphrase back what the other person just said, in one calm sentence, before you respond. It proves you heard them, drains the heat out of the moment, and buys you a half-second to answer deliberately instead of react.

Exercise: Today, the next three times someone pushes back on you — a customer questioning a range rule, a student second-guessing an instruction, one of your kids arguing a point — pause and paraphrase their position back to them before you reply. Notice if their tone drops.
