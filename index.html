Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's heated, reflect their statement back in fewer words before you respond — it proves you heard them, drops their guard, and buys you a half-second to choose your next move instead of just reacting to theirs.

Exercise: Today, in your next three tense or loaded conversations — a customer at the counter, an employee, one of your kids — pause and say back what they just told you in one sentence before you answer. Notice if their volume or tone drops.
