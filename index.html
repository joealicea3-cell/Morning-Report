Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone pushes back on a rule or decision, don't lead with 'no.' Lead with a reflected statement that shows you heard them, then give them a choice instead of a command — people comply faster when they feel like they picked the exit instead of being shoved out the door.

Exercise: Today, every time you'd normally say 'no' or 'that's not how we do it' to a customer, student, or one of the kids, swap it for 'I hear you — here's what we can do instead,' followed by two real options. Do it at least three times and notice if the pushback drops.
