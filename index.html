Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's heated, don't meet the demand head-on. Reflect their words back before you respond ('So what you're telling me is...') so they feel heard first. People drop their guard once they know you're actually listening, and that's when you can steer.

Exercise: Next time someone pushes back on you today, a customer, a kid, a vendor, pause and paraphrase their point back before you answer. Count how many times you do it and notice if the temperature in the room drops.
