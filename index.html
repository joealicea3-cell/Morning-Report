Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes in hot, acknowledge the emotion before you correct the facts. Swap 'but' for 'and': 'I hear you, and here's what we can do' keeps the other person from feeling shut down and keeps the conversation moving instead of escalating.

Exercise: Today, in every tense conversation (counter, phone, one of the kids), catch yourself before you say 'but' and replace it with 'and' at least three times. Notice if the other person de-escalates faster.
