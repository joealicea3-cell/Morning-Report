Verbal Judo

Tip: Before you respond to someone who's heated, paraphrase their concern back to them in your own words and get a nod or a 'yeah, exactly' before you say anything else - people stop escalating once they feel heard, even if the answer is still no.

Exercise: Today, pick one conversation at the range where someone's frustrated or pushing back (a customer, a student, one of the kids) and force yourself to paraphrase their point back before you reply - do it at least twice and notice if the temperature drops.
