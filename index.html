Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes at you hot, resist the urge to fix or correct in the first breath. Reflect their words back first ('Sounds like you're frustrated because...') before you respond with anything else. It buys you a beat and shows them they were actually heard, which is usually half the fight.

Exercise: Today, the first time a customer, student, or one of your kids gets short with you, pause three seconds and reflect their statement back before you reply. Notice whether the temperature in the room drops. Do it at least twice and mentally log what changed.
