Verbal Judo

Tip: When you have to correct someone's unsafe behavior, lead with the action and the fix, not their character — 'muzzle's drifting downrange, bring it back up' lands faster than 'you're being careless.' It keeps their ego out of the way so the safety point actually gets through.

Exercise: Next correction you give today, on the range or with the kids, mentally rewrite it as 'action plus fix' before you say it out loud, and notice if the response is faster than usual.
