Second Amendment News

  • Fifth Circuit: Second Amendment Protects Buying and Selling Guns, Not Just Owning Them
    In United States v. Deare, a 2-1 Fifth Circuit panel ruled the Second Amendment's text covers commercial firearm sales, vacating an unlicensed-dealing indictment and sending the case back to trial court. The ruling is binding precedent in Texas, Louisiana, and Mississippi, though it doesn't touch background checks.
    Investigate further: Yes — a real precedent on dealer rights is worth understanding for anyone running an FFL-adjacent business.
  • Minnesota Governor-Elect Klobuchar Vows to Push Assault Weapons Ban and Mag Limits
    Fresh off her win, Amy Klobuchar says she expects a sweeping gun-control package — banning AR-15-style rifles and capping magazines at 17 rounds — to pass once she takes office in January. It's part of a broader wave of gun-control pushes Democrats are planning across eight states after their midterm sweep.
    Investigate further: Yes — signals where state-level gun politics are trending nationally, worth watching even outside Minnesota.
  • Fifth Circuit Strikes Down Federal Switchblade Ban as Unconstitutional
    The same circuit ruled Section 1242 of the Federal Switchblade Act violates the Second Amendment, reasoning that switchblades are commonly used arms and the right to use them includes the right to make and acquire them. It's another plain-text win expanding what counts as a protected 'arm.'
    Investigate further: No — interesting precedent-building but doesn't touch firearms retail or training directly.
