Verbal Judo

Tip: Paraphrase before you push back. When someone's heated at you, restate their point in your own words and ask 'is that right?' before you respond. It proves you heard them, slows the exchange down, and usually drains the anger before you even get to your answer.

Exercise: Today, in your next three conversations where someone pushes back on you - a customer, an employee, one of the kids - pause and paraphrase their point before you reply. Notice how many times it changes the temperature of the room.
