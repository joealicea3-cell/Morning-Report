Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes in hot, don't jump straight to logic or a fix — reflect back what they said first ('So what you're telling me is...') before you redirect. People stop fighting you the moment they feel heard; skip that step and your best argument bounces right off them.

Exercise: In one tense conversation today — a customer complaint, a kid pushing back, staff friction — wait a full three seconds after they stop talking, then open your response by paraphrasing their point before you say anything else.
