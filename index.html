Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's escalating at the counter or on the range floor, don't lead with a command. Lead with the 'feel, felt, found' move: acknowledge what they're feeling, note that others have felt the same, then hand them the found solution. It lets them save face while you still get compliance.

Exercise: Today, catch three moments where you'd normally just bark an instruction — a customer arguing about a safety rule, a stalling employee, a kid dragging feet on cleanup. Before you respond, run it silently: acknowledge, redirect, confirm. Say the acknowledgment out loud before the correction, then check tonight whether the pushback was shorter than usual.
