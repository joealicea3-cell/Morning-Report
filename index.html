Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes at you hot — an angry customer, a student who thinks a safety call was unfair — don't respond on their clock. Take a full two-second pause before you speak. It reads as control, not hesitation, and it stops you from matching their heat.

Exercise: Today, find one moment of friction — even small, like someone questioning a range rule — and deliberately count to two in your head before responding. Notice whether the pause changes their tone as much as yours.
