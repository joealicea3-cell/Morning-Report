Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes at you hot, don't match their volume or jump to correct them. Reflect back what you heard first — 'So what you're saying is...' — before you respond. It proves you're listening and drops their guard, which is the whole game: redirect the energy, don't block it head-on.

Exercise: Today, in one heated moment with a student, employee, or one of your kids, paraphrase their point back to them before you answer — twice if you can — and notice whether the temperature drops faster than usual.
