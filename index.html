Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes in hot, resist the urge to correct them first. Paraphrase their complaint back in your own words so they know they were heard before you respond. People stop escalating once they feel understood.

Exercise: In your next tense moment today, whether it's a customer, a kid, or a vendor, pause three seconds and say 'So what you're telling me is...' before you say anything else. Watch what it does to their shoulders.
