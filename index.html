Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's voice goes up, let yours go down. Dropping your volume and slowing your pace signals control without saying a word, and it gives the other person room to come down off their ledge instead of matching your energy and climbing higher.

Exercise: Next time someone on the range or at home gets loud or heated today, deliberately cut your own volume by half and slow your speech before you respond. Notice how many seconds it takes them to mirror you back down.
