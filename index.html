Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's voice starts climbing, don't match it - drop your volume and ask a genuine question instead of firing back a statement. It forces them to explain instead of escalate, and it puts you back in control of the pace.

Exercise: Next time you feel your jaw tighten today - with a customer, an employee, or one of the kids - pause two full seconds, then respond with a question instead of a comeback. Do it at least once and notice what changes in their tone.
