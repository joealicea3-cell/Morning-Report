Verbal Judo

Tip: Use the ask-tell-make ladder: open with a respectful ask, calmly tell if that's ignored, and only make (enforce or remove) as the last resort - most people fall in line at 'ask' once they feel respected instead of ordered.

Exercise: Today, catch yourself before barking a correction - at the range, with the kids, wherever - and deliver it as one respectful ask first. Count how many times 'ask' alone gets the job done without you ever reaching 'tell.'
