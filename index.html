Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's voice speeds up and gets louder, don't match their energy, mismatch it: drop your volume and slow your cadence. It signals you're not a threat and gives them room to de-escalate themselves instead of digging in.

Exercise: Today, the next time you feel your own voice start to rise, whether on the range floor, on the phone, or with the kids, deliberately cut your volume in half and add a one-second pause before you respond. Watch what the other person does in the next 10 seconds.
