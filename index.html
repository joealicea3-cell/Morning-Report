Verbal Judo

Tip: Verbal Judo's core move is redirect, don't resist: when someone comes in hot, don't block the attitude head-on. Absorb it with a calm, respectful line like 'I hear you, help me understand what happened,' then steer the energy toward the actual problem instead of the ego fight.
