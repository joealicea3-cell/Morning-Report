Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes at you hot — an angry customer, a vendor, one of the kids — don't match their pace. Let a full two-second beat of silence pass before you respond. Silence reads as control, not weakness, and it forces the other person to slow down to meet you.

Exercise: Today, pick one tense or heated moment as it happens and consciously count 'one-one-thousand, two-one-thousand' in your head before you speak. Afterward, jot one line tonight on how the outcome changed compared to your usual instant response.
