Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's escalating, don't match their volume or argue the facts first — mirror their emotion back in one calm sentence, like 'sounds like this really pissed you off,' before you pivot to the fix. People can't hear a solution until they feel heard.

Exercise: Next time someone at the range gets heated today, whether it's a student, a customer, or an employee, practice paraphrase-then-pivot: repeat their concern back in your own words, then immediately say 'here's what we're going to do about it.' Run it at least once, even on something small like a scheduling gripe.
