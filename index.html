Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes at you hot, mirror their last few words back in a calm, flat tone before you respond (e.g., 'sounds like you're saying the wait time's the problem') - it proves you heard them and buys a beat before either of you escalates.

Exercise: Today, in one conversation where you expect pushback - an employee, a customer, or one of the kids - use the mirror technique at least twice before you state your own position, and notice whether their tone drops.
