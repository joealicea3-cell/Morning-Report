Verbal Judo

Tip: Before you respond to someone who's heated, paraphrase what they just said back to them in your own words. It costs you nothing, proves you actually heard them, and it's hard for someone to stay at a boil when they feel understood instead of argued with.

Exercise: Today, in the first disagreement you hit — a customer complaint, an employee excuse, one of the kids talking back — paraphrase their point back before you give yours. Do it three times over the day and notice whether it changes how fast the conversation cools off.
