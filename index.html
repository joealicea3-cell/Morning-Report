Verbal Judo

Tip: Before you respond to someone who's heated (a customer complaint, an employee mouthing off, one of your kids pushing back), paraphrase what they just said back to them first -- 'So what you're telling me is...' It proves you heard them, buys you a beat before you react, and takes most of the air out of the fight before it starts.

Exercise: Today, in your next three tense conversations, paraphrase before you reply -- no exceptions, even if you think you already know what they're going to say. Notice how much faster the temperature drops when people feel heard before they get answered.

Vocabulary

Velleity (From the Latin "velle," meaning "to wish" or "to will" -- entered English in the early 1600s)
A wish or inclination too weak to actually lead to action; wanting something without doing anything about it.
Talking about expanding the range someday is a velleity until you put a number and a date on it -- that's the whole difference between trying and doing.
