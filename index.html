Second Amendment News

  • DOJ Says Federal Ban on Handgun Sales to 18-to-20-Year-Olds Is Unconstitutional
    The DOJ's Office of Legal Counsel issued an opinion this week backing the Fifth Circuit's ruling that the federal ban on licensed dealers selling handguns to 18-to-20-year-olds violates the Second Amendment. DOJ's brief laying out that position is due to the Fifth Circuit on September 21.
    Investigate further: yes, if this holds up it changes who can legally buy a handgun and train at the range
  • Supreme Court's September 28 Conference Stacked With Second Amendment Cases
    The Court's long conference on September 28 has several major 2A cases queued up, including large-capacity magazine bans out of California and Connecticut (Duncan, Gator's Custom Guns, NAGR v. Lamont), setting up what could be the heaviest Second Amendment docket in years for the term opening in October.
