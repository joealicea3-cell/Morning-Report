Verbal Judo

Tip: Lead with empathy before authority: when a customer or student gets defensive about a safety correction or a billing dispute, reflect back what you heard them say before you redirect to the rule. People drop their guard and comply faster once they feel heard, not managed.

Exercise: Next tense conversation today, run the LEAPS sequence out loud in your head before you respond: Listen, Empathize, Ask a clarifying question, Paraphrase what they said, then Summarize the resolution. Notice whether the other person de-escalates faster than usual.
