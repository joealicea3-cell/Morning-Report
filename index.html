Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes at you hot, don't match their volume or their pace. Drop your voice half a notch and open with a genuine question like 'help me understand what happened' before you offer any fix. It forces them to shift from venting to explaining, and buys you time to actually assess what's going on instead of reacting to the heat.

Exercise: Today, pick the next person who comes to you frustrated, whether it's a student on the range, a customer, or one of the kids, and practice the pause: let them finish completely, then respond with a question instead of a statement. Do it three separate times before the day's out and notice how differently the conversation moves.
