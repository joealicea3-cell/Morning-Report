Verbal Judo

Tip: Before you redirect or correct someone, give them the 'empathy before authority' move: name what they're feeling or trying to do out loud first ('I can see you're trying to muscle through that grip') before you give the fix. People drop their guard and actually hear the correction once they feel seen.

Exercise: Today, pick one correction you have to make -- with a student, an employee, or one of your kids -- and run it in two steps: (1) state back what they're going for in your own words, (2) then give the one-sentence fix. No lecture, no stacking. See if it lands faster than your usual way.
