Verbal Judo

Tip: When a customer or student gets heated, name the emotion out loud before you address the facts ('I can see you're frustrated with the wait') — it signals you heard them and drops their defensiveness before you redirect to the fix.

Exercise: Today, pick one interaction where someone's annoyed or short with you -- staff, a customer, one of the kids -- and open your response by naming their emotion before you say anything else. Notice if their shoulders drop before you even get to your point.
