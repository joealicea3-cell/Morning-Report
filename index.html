Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's heated at the counter or on the range, lead with an empathy statement before you redirect -- 'I hear you, that's frustrating' -- then give them a clear choice of two acceptable next steps instead of a flat no. It lets them save face while you still control the outcome.

Exercise: Today, in your next three mildly tense conversations (a customer complaint, a kid's excuse, an employee pushback), force yourself to say one empathy line out loud before you respond with the actual answer. Notice if their shoulders drop before you even get to the fix.
