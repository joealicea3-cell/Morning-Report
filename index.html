Verbal Judo

Tip: Ditch the word 'why' when someone's already defensive — it lands like an accusation and makes people dig in. Swap it for 'what' or 'how' to keep them talking instead of justifying.

Exercise: Today, catch yourself once with an employee, student, or one of your kids before you ask 'why did you do that?' — rephrase it to 'what happened?' or 'how'd that play out?' and notice if they open up instead of getting defensive.
