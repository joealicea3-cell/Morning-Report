Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's heated, label the emotion you see before you address the content: 'Sounds like you're pretty frustrated about this' lands better than jumping straight to the fix or the correction, because it tells them you heard them before you try to redirect them.

Exercise: Today, the first time a customer, employee, or one of the kids comes at you hot about something, pause and name what you see ('sounds like you're annoyed/worried/ticked off about X') before you say anything else. Notice whether their shoulders drop before you've even gotten to your answer.
