Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone gets defensive or heated, don't jump straight to correcting them - mirror back what they said in your own words first ('sounds like you're saying...') before you respond. It proves you heard them, which lowers their guard before you redirect.

Exercise: Today, the next time a student, employee, or customer pushes back on something, pause and paraphrase their concern back to them in one sentence before you answer. Do it at least twice and notice how much faster the tension drops.
