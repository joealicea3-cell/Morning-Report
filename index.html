Verbal Judo

Tip: Aim for 'that's right,' not 'you're right' - paraphrase what the other person just said back to them in your own words until they confirm you actually got it. Agreement earned that way lowers a person's guard faster than any argument does.

Exercise: Next time a customer, staff member, or one of your kids is heated about something today, don't defend or explain first - summarize their complaint back in one sentence and wait for them to say 'that's right' before you respond.
