Verbal Judo

Tip: Swap 'but' for 'and' when you're redirecting someone who's heated -- 'but' erases everything they just said, 'and' builds on it and keeps them listening instead of squaring up.

Exercise: Today, catch yourself three times before saying 'but' in a disagreement -- with a customer, a kid, an employee, doesn't matter -- and replace it with 'and here's what I'd add.' Notice whether their shoulders drop instead of tensing.
