Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes in hot, don't match their energy, mirror their words first. A quick, calm paraphrase of what they just said ('sounds like you're saying...') buys you a beat and shows respect before you ever get to your point, which usually deflates the tension on its own.

Exercise: Today, the next time someone pushes back on you, whether it's a customer, a vendor, or one of the kids, pause three seconds, repeat their point back in one sentence, then respond. Do it at least twice and pay attention to whether their tone drops.
