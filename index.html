Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's heating up - an angry customer, a corrected employee, one of the kids mid-meltdown - narrate the observable behavior back to them in a flat, neutral tone before you respond to the content of what they said ('Your voice just got louder and you're leaning over the counter'). It interrupts the escalation loop without you having to argue or agree, because you're not fighting their point, you're just naming what's happening.

Exercise: Today, catch one moment of rising tension - at the range, at home, wherever - and use the 'say what you see' line before you say anything else. Notice whether their volume or posture drops before you even get to your actual response.
