Verbal Judo

Tip: Reflect the other person's words back to them before you respond ('So what you're telling me is...') — it buys you a beat to think, proves you're actually listening, and drains tension out of a conversation faster than arguing your point first.

Exercise: In your next three conversations today — a student, a vendor, one of the kids — paraphrase what they said back to them before you reply. Notice whether their tone softens before you even make your point.
