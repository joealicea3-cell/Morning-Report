Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes in hot, don't argue the facts first — label the emotion you're seeing out loud ('sounds like you're frustrated this took longer than it should have') before you respond to their complaint. People drop their guard when they feel heard instead of fought.

Exercise: Next pushback you get today, whether it's a customer unhappy about a class or one of your kids arguing about chores, mirror their last few words back as a question, then name the emotion. Go quiet for three full seconds after and let them fill the silence before you make your point.
