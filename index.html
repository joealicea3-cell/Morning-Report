Verbal Judo

Tip: Before you answer a hostile or defensive customer, silently name what's actually driving them (fear, ego, confusion) and respond to that root cause instead of arguing their exact words — it de-escalates faster than winning the surface complaint.

Exercise: Pick one tense conversation today, with a customer, a vendor, or one of your kids, and use the 'paraphrase-then-redirect' move: repeat their concern back in one sentence before you respond. Do it at least twice and notice if their tone softens.
