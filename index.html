Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's heating up, trade commands for choices. Telling a person to 'calm down' or 'stop' makes them defend their position; instead say 'Here's what I can do, you can do X or Y, which works better for you?' Options let them cooperate without losing face.

Exercise: Today, catch yourself right before you bark an order, at a nervous new shooter, an employee, or one of your kids, and rephrase it as two options instead of one command. Notice how much less pushback you get.
