Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's voice goes up, drop yours down and slow it down. A calm, unhurried tone breaks the emotional momentum faster than any argument does, and on the range that half-second reset is often the difference between a correction landing clean and a fight starting.

Exercise: In every pushback conversation today, even a low-stakes one, pause two full seconds and repeat back what you heard the other person say in one sentence before you respond. Do it at least twice so it's automatic the next time the stakes are real.
