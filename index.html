Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes in hot (a frustrated customer, an upset parent, a heated employee issue), lower your own voice and slow your pace before you respond -- it forces the other person to match your tempo instead of you matching their volume.

Exercise: Today, in the next tense conversation you have, deliberately pause for a full two seconds before replying and answer one register quieter than the other person. Notice if it changes their tone within the first minute.
