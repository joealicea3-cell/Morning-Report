Verbal Judo

Tip: Lead with empathy before you redirect. When a customer, student, or kid does something unsafe or frustrating, acknowledge what they were trying to do before you correct the behavior - it drops their defenses and gets faster compliance than a flat order.

Exercise: Today, the next time someone on the range or at home does something that irritates you, pause three seconds and open with 'I hear you, here's what we're doing instead' before giving the instruction. Do it at least three times and notice whether the pushback is lighter than usual.
