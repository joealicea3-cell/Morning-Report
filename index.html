Verbal Judo

Tip: Before you respond to someone who's heated, reflect back what you heard in one sentence before adding your own point — it slows the exchange down and proves you're actually listening, which drains most of the anger out of a confrontation.

Exercise: Today, in at least three conversations (a customer, an employee, one of your kids), pause and say 'So what you're telling me is...' before you give your own response, and notice if the tone shifts.
