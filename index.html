Verbal Judo

Tip: Lead with empathy before correction. When someone's heated — a customer misreading a range rule, a nervous new shooter getting defensive, a dad arguing a safety call you made about his kid — open with 'I hear you, and here's what I need from you,' instead of 'No, you can't.' It gives them a way to save face while the line still holds.

Exercise: Today, every time you have to correct someone — staff, a student on the line, or one of your own four — say the empathy half out loud before the correction. Count how many times you catch yourself skipping straight to the correction instead.
