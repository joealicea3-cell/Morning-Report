Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's voice speeds up and gets louder, don't match it — deliberately drop your volume and slow your own pace by half a beat. The mismatch breaks the escalation loop and pulls them down toward your tempo instead of you getting pulled up into theirs.

Exercise: Today, pick one moment where someone's irritated or amped up (a customer, a kid, a vendor on the phone) and consciously answer a notch quieter and slower than they came in. Notice whether their next sentence comes down to meet you.
