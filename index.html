  • Fifth Circuit Strikes Down Federal Ban on Interstate Handgun Sales
    A 2-1 panel ruled in Elite Precision Customs v. ATF that the 1968 ban on direct interstate handgun purchases violates the Second Amendment, siding with the Firearms Policy Coalition and reversing the lower court. The ruling could reshape how FFLs handle cross-state handgun sales.
    Investigate further: yes — directly touches how the range/academy could sell or transfer handguns across state lines; worth confirming what actually changes in practice
  • Trump Ordered DOJ Not to Appeal Ruling That Deregulated Suppressors, Short-Barreled Guns
    CBS News reports Trump personally directed DOJ to let stand an August ruling by a Texas federal judge striking down NFA registration requirements for suppressors, SBRs, SBSs and AOWs, after Congress zeroed out the related tax stamps. The change currently applies to the named parties, not nationwide.
    Investigate further: yes — big implications for suppressor/NFA sales and classes if it broadens; worth tracking before any inventory moves
