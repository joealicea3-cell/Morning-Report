Verbal Judo

Tip: Ask before you assert. When someone's heated or pushing back on a range rule, open with a genuine question ('walk me through what you're seeing') before you state the policy. It signals respect, buys you a read on them, and usually cools the temperature before you ever have to say 'no.'

Exercise: Next time today someone disputes a rule or instruction — customer, staff, or one of the kids — catch yourself before the correction and ask one open question first. Notice how their tone shifts before you say your piece.
