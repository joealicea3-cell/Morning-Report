Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes at you hot, don't meet force with force. Greet the emotion first with a genuine reflective line like "I hear you, that's frustrating" before you say another word about the actual problem. That single beat of acknowledgment drains most of the heat before you ever get to the solution.

Exercise: Today, the next time a customer, employee, or one of the kids comes to you frustrated or short, pause three seconds, reflect their words back in your own ("sounds like you feel..."), then offer exactly one option instead of arguing your point. Do it at least twice and notice how fast the temperature drops.
