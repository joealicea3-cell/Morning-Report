Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes in hot - an angry customer, a pushback from staff, one of your kids mid-meltdown - reflect their point back in your own words before you respond. It proves you heard them and buys you a beat to choose your next move on purpose instead of reacting.

Exercise: Today, in the next two tense conversations you hit, pause and say 'so what you're telling me is...' before you give your answer. Notice whether the other person's shoulders drop when they feel heard - that's the tell it worked.
