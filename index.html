Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone comes at you hot, don't block it head-on - redirect the energy. Acknowledge what they said first ('I hear you, that's frustrating') before you pivot to your response. It lowers their guard instead of raising it, same principle as the martial art the technique is named after.

Exercise: Pick one interaction today - a customer, a student on the range, or one of your kids - where you'd normally jump straight to your answer. Instead, lead with a one-line acknowledgment of what they said before responding. Do it at least three times before the day's over and notice how the other person's tone shifts.
