    Investigate further: yes - could open new lawful sales channels for FFLs and range customers buying from out-of-state dealers; worth understanding before customers start asking
  • Trump personally blocks DOJ appeal of ruling that loosens NFA limits on suppressors and short-barreled guns
    A Texas federal judge ruled in August that NFA registration requirements are unconstitutional for suppressors, short-barreled rifles and shotguns since Congress zeroed out the $200 transfer tax; Trump has told Republicans he'll keep DOJ from appealing before the October 5 deadline.

  • Supreme Court sets December 2 arguments in consolidated AR-15 ban cases
    The Court will hear Viramontes v. Cook County and Grant v. Higgins together to decide whether the Second and Fourteenth Amendments protect AR-15-style semiautomatic rifles, with the Trump DOJ arguing on the side of gun owners.
    Investigate further: yes - a ruling here could reshape what's legal to sell and teach with nationwide; the kind of case worth planning around, not just reading about

Verbal Judo

Tip: Before you respond to someone who's heated, paraphrase their concern back to them in one sentence before you say anything else. It proves you actually heard them, and most people's guard drops the moment they feel understood instead of managed.

Exercise: Today, in your next three tense or important conversations -- a nervous new shooter, a difficult customer, one of the kids pushing back -- pause and open your response with 'What I hear you saying is...' before you give your answer. Notice how much faster things settle.
