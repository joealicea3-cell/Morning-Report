Verbal Judo

Tip: When a customer or student gets loud or defensive, don't match their tone — open with a genuine empathy statement ('I hear you, that's frustrating') before you redirect to what you need from them. It costs you nothing and it disarms people faster than logic does.

Exercise: Today, the next time someone pushes back on you (a customer, a kid, an employee), pause two full seconds before responding, then start your reply with 'I understand' or 'I hear you' before you say anything else. Do it at least three times and notice how the conversation changes direction.
