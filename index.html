Second Amendment News

  • Fifth Circuit: Second Amendment Covers Buying and Selling Guns
    A divided Fifth Circuit panel ruled Nov. 19 in United States v. Deare that the Second Amendment's text protects commercial firearm sales, not just ownership, sending a Louisiana dealer-licensing case back for further review. The ruling is binding precedent across Texas, Louisiana, and Mississippi.
    Investigate further: yes - a published circuit-level win on gun commerce is the kind of precedent that eventually reshapes FFL and dealer law nationwide; worth understanding the reasoning
  • Supreme Court Passes on Maryland's 'Gun-Free Zone' Law, Thomas Signals More Fights Ahead
    SCOTUS declined to hear a challenge to Maryland's law barring concealed carry in schools, transit, bars, and other 'sensitive places,' leaving the restriction in place for now. Justice Thomas wrote separately, signaling he wants a narrower case to take up the issue later.
    Investigate further: no - doesn't change law outside Maryland, but it shows where the Court's appetite currently sits on 'sensitive places' laws
  • ATF's Suppressor Deregulation Faces Political Pushback
    Since October, ATF has stopped enforcing NFA registration and tax-stamp requirements for suppressors, short-barreled rifles, and shotguns after a Texas federal court ruling, letting licensed dealers sell them under ordinary background-check rules. Critics note it's only an enforcement policy, not a repeal, and Democrats are already signaling they'd restore the old rules if they retake the executive branch.
    Investigate further: yes - directly touches what you can sell, teach, and demo at the range; worth confirming your compliance posture before policy swings back
