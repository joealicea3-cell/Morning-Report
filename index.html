Verbal Judo

Tip: Open with an empathy statement before you correct anyone - 'I hear you, and here's what we're going to do' - instead of jumping straight to 'no.' It disarms the defensiveness before the correction even lands.

Exercise: Today, the next three times you have to correct a student on the line, redirect a staff member, or referee a squabble between the kids, start the sentence with 'I understand, and...' before you deliver the correction. Notice whether the pushback drops.
