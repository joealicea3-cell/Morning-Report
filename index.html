Verbal Judo

Tip: When someone's voice starts climbing — a nervous new shooter, a frustrated customer arguing a safety call — never say 'calm down,' it always backfires. Mirror their concern back first ('I hear you, this matters, let's fix it') before you redirect them to what you actually need. That's the judo: take their energy and turn it, don't block it head-on.

Exercise: At least once today, even in a low-stakes moment (a kid, a vendor call, a staff disagreement), consciously run the sequence out loud in your head before responding: Empathize, then Redirect, then one clear instruction. Reps in low-stakes moments make it reflexive for the moment it actually matters on the range floor.
